Summer is a great time to have fun! No school, no tests, and no homework!


But summer can be a dangerous time, too. Read about these common summer dangers, then answer this question: Do you think these common things are really that dangerous?

1 / 7


Outdoor Sports


489,000


About 489,000 people globally die each year from the heat. Drink lots of water and take a lot of breaks when you're doing outdoor sports!

2 / 7


Swimming


300,000


Around 300,000 people die each year globally from drowning in seas, rivers, and lakes. Swimming is a lot of fun but be careful, especially if you're not swimming in a swimming pool!

3 / 7


Trampolines and Playgrounds


300,000


Over 300,000 people go to the hospital each year in the USA after falling from trampolines and playground equipment. Have fun, but watch out!

4 / 7


Pets


86,000


About 86,000 people in the USA are injured after tripping and falling over a pet or pet toy. Watch where you step!

5 / 7


Cycling


50,000


Around 50,000 cyclists die in accidents each year in the world. Make sure to wear your helmet!
